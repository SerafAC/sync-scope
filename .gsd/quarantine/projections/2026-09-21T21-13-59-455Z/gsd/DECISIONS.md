| D008 | M001 Layer 2 architecture discussion | architecture | How local deletion is executed | Two-phase: prepareLocalDeletion returns a plan token plus a breakdown of synced, unsynced-warned, and unknown-refused counts; executeLocalDeletion(planToken) commits with per-file outcomes written to local_deletion_overlay on success only | Deletion is irreversible and the whole app exists to drive it.
